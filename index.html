Built to keep your data in Jira

The app runs on Atlassian Forge, inside Atlassian's cloud. We do not run our own servers and cannot see your Jira data or tokens.

Read the privacy policy →
  • Read-only access to Jira
  • Bot tokens and webhooks kept in encrypted Forge storage
  • Issues with a security level are never posted
  • No analytics, no tracking, no sale of data
